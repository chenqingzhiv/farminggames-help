# 🌿 Tiny Eden Guides

> Bajka Games & Digital Vortex Entertainment's cozy indoor farming sim | Releasing **September 7, 2026** (Steam PC) | Free demo available now

**Tiny Eden** is the coziest idea in farming games this year: a first-person indoor farming sim set in a bare apartment in a near-future (2050) megacity. Your entire "farm" fits inside four walls — one windowsill pot at first, then a whole room, then the rooftop. It plays like Stardew if Stardew happened on your balcony, with a deliberately realistic growing system: soil chemistry, water amounts, distinct growth phases and indoor temperature all matter. You grow, cook, sell or deliver to neighbors, build reputation with the apartment's DIY Club, automate with irrigation, decorate your space, and care for a cat.

## The Hook

Most farming games hand you a field and a hoe. **Tiny Eden** hands you a seed packet, a windowsill, and a gray megacity outside the window — then asks you to turn an empty apartment into a living green space, one pot at a time. With a genuinely tactile growing system (your peppers *will* complain if the room gets too hot), a free demo that's already sitting at ~91% positive, and a cat, it's the rare cozy game where the small scale is the whole point.
