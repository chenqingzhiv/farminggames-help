# 🌘 Village in the Shade Guides

> Nippon Ichi Software's rural life sim by the Yomawari team | Japan: July 30, 2026 · West: Fall 2026 | PC (Steam), PS5, Switch, Switch 2

**Village in the Shade** (ほの暮しの庭 / *Hono Gurashi no Niwa*) is a cozy farming sim with a secret: the remote village of Kagatsu is governed by eight rules, and breaking them lets the *Yomawari* horror in. Farm 80+ crops by day, then explore the village's mysteries at night.
