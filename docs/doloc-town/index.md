# 🌱 Doloc Town Guides

> RedSaw Games Studio's post-apocalyptic farming sim | 1.0 released August 6, 2026 | PC (Steam) | "Overwhelmingly Positive" (95%)

**Doloc Town** (*多洛可小镇*) is a side-scrolling farming and survival sim set in a wasteland eaten by acid rain and scorching sun. You play a scavenger who walks into a run-down settlement and turns it into a working farm — by day you till soil, water crops, and fish the Deer God pool; by night you upgrade your combat drone and fight your way through mines and ruins. The full 1.0 launched on **August 6, 2026** with the story's ending, a farming automation system, a third map, and dozens of new fish and achievements.

## The Hook

Most farming games ask you to build a farm in a nice place. **Doloc Town** asks you to build one in a wasteland where the weather is the main boss. Acid rain will eat your crops if you don't cover them; a scorching sun will dry out your soil. The game's whole loop is *scavenge → plant → protect → sell → upgrade* — and once you automate with drones, the farm mostly runs itself while you go fight monsters with a flying combat drone.
