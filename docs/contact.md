# 📧 Contact Us

Have a question, found an error, or want to request a new guide? We'd love to hear from you. Here's how to reach us:

## Report an Error or Suggest a Fix

Every guide on this site is **hand-checked** against in-game data, but games update constantly and mistakes happen. If you spot something wrong:

- **Open an issue**: [github.com/chenqingzhiv/farminggames-help/issues](https://github.com/chenqingzhiv/farminggames-help/issues)
- **Use the feedback widget**: every game page has a 📝 feedback widget at the bottom — rate the page and leave a comment.

## Request a New Guide or Tool

Want us to cover a new game, a specific crop table, or a new calculator? Let us know:

- **GitHub profile**: [github.com/chenqingzhiv](https://github.com/chenqingzhiv)
- **Issue tracker**: open a [feature request](https://github.com/chenqingzhiv/farminggames-help/issues)

## Business & Advertising Inquiries

For advertising, partnership, or affiliate inquiries, please use the contact channels above. We're always open to working with developers and brands that fit the farming/simulation gaming community.

## Response Time

We typically respond within **1–3 business days**. Please include as much detail as possible (game, version, page URL) so we can help you faster.
