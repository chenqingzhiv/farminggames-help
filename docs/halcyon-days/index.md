# 🏮 Halcyon Days at Taoyuan Guides

> BotanX's Chinese folklore farming sim | Released January 20, 2026 | PC (Steam) | "Very Positive" (89%)

**Halcyon Days at Taoyuan** (*桃源村日志*) is a cozy farming and life sim set in Taoyuan Village — a hidden village straight out of the *Peach Blossom Spring* (桃花源记) fable, cut off from the outside world for a thousand years. You play an outsider who lands in the village and takes up a farm, then slowly pulls it into the community: trading by barter, exploring caves, befriending pandas, racing dragon boats, and even getting married in a full Chinese-style wedding. It's been described as "the Stardew Valley of Chinese folklore," and the recent **V1.1 flower update** (June 2026) plus the **V1.1.1 patch** (late July 2026) have kept it growing all summer.

## The Hook

Most farming games give you a shipping bin and a wallet. **Halcyon Days at Taoyuan throws both away** — there's no currency in the village at all. Everything runs on barter: every item has a hidden **Contribution Value**, and to get something you offer items whose values add up to exactly what you want. A tree you chop becomes the trade-good that buys your first tool upgrades; donating to the village earns vouchers worth a tenth of an item's value. It's a small, elegant twist that makes every foraging trip feel like a real trade route — and it's the single thing that makes this game feel unlike any other cozy farming sim.
