# 🏘️ Go-Go Town! Guides

> Prideful Sloth's cozy town-builder + farming sim | Launched July 16, 2026 (Steam 1.0, after two years of Early Access) | PC · Switch · Switch 2 | $27.99 ($20.99 launch week)

**Go-Go Town!** is a cozy-but-deep city-building and life-sim game where you play a hands-on mayor rebuilding a derelict town for TownCo. Unlike pure farming sims, most of the work can be handed to NPC workers — so your real job is designing production lines, routing resources and laying out districts. The **1.0 release** adds online co-op (up to **4 players** on Steam), a refreshed **tech tree**, Creative Mode, Treasure Hunting and the Import app.

## The Hook

Most cozy builders hand you a town and let you decorate. **Go-Go Town!** hands you a town, a budget, and a worker shortage — and quietly turns into an automation puzzle the moment you hire a second person. It's the farming sim where Stardew's vibes meet Anno's supply chains, and the 1.0 release finally delivers online co-op for four, a proper tech tree, and a real endgame.
