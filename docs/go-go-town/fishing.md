# Go-Go Town! Fishing Guide — How to Catch Every Fish

> Game: Go-Go Town! (狗狗镇) | Developer: Prideful Sloth | Release: July 16, 2026 (Steam 1.0, after two years of Early Access) | Platforms: PC (Steam), Nintendo Switch, Switch 2

Fishing in Go-Go Town! is more than a peaceful way to spend an afternoon—it's a key part of the Treasure Hunting system, a source of rare loot, and a reliable way to boost your early-game income. Since the full 1.0 release in July 2026, fishing has been integrated into town goals and collection rewards, making it a worthwhile grind for any aspiring mayor. ## Where to Fish in Each District

Fishing spots are scattered across the map, but not all are created equal. As of patch 1.0, there are two primary types of fishing spots you'll encounter: **Standard Fishing Spots** and **Junk Fishing Spots**.

### Standard Fishing Spots

- **Fishing Zone:** The go-to location for standard catches. This is where you'll find the Octowurst Fishing Spot, a unique generator that yields the elusive Octowurst.

### Junk Fishing Spots

Junk spots are where you'll find the weird, wonderful, and often more valuable items. These generators appear in specific biomes and have a key difference from standard spots: once you fish a junk spot, it disappears and requires time before another one generates in the area. This makes grinding for rare junk items a game of patience and map awareness.

Here is the confirmed breakdown of where you can find Junk Fishing Spots:

- **Forest & Deep Forest:** These wooded areas are prime real estate for junk spots. Several players report that the Deep Forest is the most reliable location for catching rare fossils like the Big Bone, though it is complete RNG and can take days of in-game grinding.
- **Petal Grove:** Another biome where junk fishing spots spawn, offering a different pool of potential loot.
- **Sewer:** As of the Creative Corners major update, junk fishing spots are now available in the Sewer. This is a newer addition, so keep an eye on patch notes for any tweaks to its loot pool.

If you're playing in co-op, remember that fishing spots are shared between players. Coordinate with your friends to avoid competing for the same junk spots, especially in the Deep Forest where they can be scarce.

## Rod, Gear, and Upgrades

While there is no complex "meta" for rod upgrades in the current patch notes, the 1.0 update revised the game's progression systems significantly. The best way to improve your fishing efficiency is through two main avenues:

1. **Tech Tree Upgrades:** The redesigned Tech Tree introduced in 1.0 allows you to unlock new perks and abilities. Although specific fishing-related nodes haven't been detailed, the new system offers rewards at each Town Ranking tier, which likely includes efficiency bonuses for resource gathering tasks like fishing.
2. **Automation with Workers:** Once you unlock the right infrastructure, you can assign workers to the Fishing Zone. Give them a rod, and they can grind for you while you focus on other town projects. This is particularly useful for passively generating common fish and loot while you sleep or explore other districts.

## Fishing Town Goals and Collection Rewards

Fishing is deeply tied to the **Treasure Hunting** system introduced in version 1.0. Catching fish and other treasures fills out your Collections App, which serves as a compendium of everything you've discovered across the map. This is a major part of the endgame, as you're encouraged to explore every zone to find all the critters and treasure you can.

Once discovered, you can display your catches in **aquariums, stands, and plinths** around your town. This not only looks great but also boosts tourism by giving visitors more attractions to enjoy. It's a smart way to turn your fishing loot into passive income and town appeal.

Town Goals will occasionally ask you to catch specific items or provide fish to unlock new buildings and upgrades. As you progress towards "Number One" town status, these requests become more frequent and demanding. The endgame story has also been reworked for 1.0, so completing these goals ties directly into uncovering what's behind that mysterious door.

## Money-Making Tips & Pro Tips for Master Anglers

If you're looking to make some serious coin from fishing, here are some strategies to keep in mind:

1. **Target Junk Spots in the Deep Forest:** Rare items like the **Big Bone** can take a while to farm, but when they drop, they are worth the grind. The Deep Forest and Deep Deep Forest are the only confirmed locations, and it's complete RNG, so persistence is key.
2. **Rotate Through All Junk Spots:** Since junk spots disappear after one use and regenerate on a timer, you can maximize your efficiency by creating a route that loops through the Forest, Deep Forest, Petal Grove, and Sewer. By the time you complete the circuit, the earlier spots may have respawned.
3. **Automate the Process:** As soon as you can, add a worker to the Fishing Zone and give them a rod. They can passively generate fish and loot for you while you focus on other town projects, saving you from grinding the common catches manually.
4. **Display Rare Catches for Tourism:** Don't just sell everything. Your rarest finds—like the Tree Swing or Big Bone—can be displayed in aquariums or on stands to attract more tourists. More tourists mean more spending, which translates into long-term passive income.
5. **Play in Co-op to Split the Grind:** Fishing spots are shared resources. In co-op, one player can focus on fishing the deep forest while another tackles the sewer or petal grove. This dramatically speeds up the collection process and helps fill out your Collections App faster.
6. **Don't Sleep on Maintenance Workers:** The 1.0 update added the ability for maintenance workers to collect items left on the ground outside zones and take them to the "Lost and Found" storage. If you're fishing in bulk and dropping items to manage inventory, your workers will clean up and store them for you.
7. **Stay Patch-Savvy:** The devs have been releasing hotfixes post-1.0, including Switch-specific patches on July 31, 2026. Always check the latest patch notes to see if fish spawn rates, loot tables, or sell prices have been tweaked.

- **Rumble Feedback:** As of the 1.0.25831 patch, a rumble effect was added when a fish is hooked. Use this tactile feedback to react faster and improve your catch rate.
- **Classy Glasses:** Patch 1.0.25917 introduced a dedicated icon for Classy Glasses. While not fishing-specific, keeping your town looking sharp with these aesthetic upgrades can help attract more tourists and improve your overall town ranking.
- **Know When to Drop Your Rod:** If you accidentally pick up your rod and can't perform actions, press Q to drop it or place it back on its stand. This old bug has been largely resolved, but it's still a handy tip for any angler.
- **Explore Everything:** The game encourages you to explore new areas to unlock fast travel and find hidden zones full of items and vehicles. Some of the best fishing spots are tucked away in these hidden zones, so don't just stick to the main paths.
