# Go-Go Town! Crops Guide

Farming in *Go-Go Town!* is more than just planting seeds and waiting for rain. It is the backbone of your town's economy, a key source of ingredients for advanced recipes, and a major driver for attracting new residents. While the game blends elements of city management and automation, your farming zone is where the raw materials for your town's growth begin.

This guide will walk you through everything you need to know about the crop system. From understanding the core crops and their processing chains to planning your fields for maximum profit, you will learn how to turn your farm into a thriving, automated engine for your town.

## Getting Started with Your Farming Zone

Before you can plant your first seed, you need to establish a Farming Zone. Open the Build Menu and select the Zones tab (the icon with a flag on an orange field). From here, you can designate an area for farming. As you expand your town, you can create additional farming zones to increase your production capacity.

Once your zone is placed, you can start planting. Crops are grown from seeds, which can be purchased from the market or obtained through other means as you progress. Initially, you will do the planting and harvesting yourself, but a core part of the game's loop is automating this process. By hiring farmhands and building processing machines, you can transform your farm from a manual chore into a self-sustaining production line.

## Core Crops, Processing Chains, and Best Picks by Stage

The Farming and Animal Husbandry Area provides a wide variety of raw materials, from fruits and vegetables to animal products. Many crops are not just for selling; they are the first step in a production chain. Processing raw crops into higher-value goods is one of the most effective ways to increase your profits.

Here are the primary crops and their processing paths:

- **Wheat**: This is a foundational crop. Wheat can be processed in a Food Processor to create Flour. Flour can then be further processed in a Roaster to make Bread. Wheat and Eggplant can also be combined in a Food Processor to make Hot Dogs, which are used to supply food trucks.
- **Sugarcane**: Plant Sugarcane seeds to grow this crop. Sugarcane is processed in a Food Processor to produce Sugar, a vital ingredient for many recipes and tourist goods.
- **Peas**: Grown from Pea seeds. Peas are processed in the Milk/Soy Milk Machine to produce Soy Milk.
- **Coffee Beans**: Grown from Coffee seeds. These beans are placed in a Roaster to produce Roasted Coffee Beans, another high-value ingredient for your town's cafes and shops.
- **Grapes**: Plant Grape seeds to grow this crop. Grapes are processed in a Food Processor to produce Grape Juice.
- **Cocoa Beans**: Grown from Cocoa seeds. Cocoa Beans are processed in the Milk/Soy Milk Machine to produce Chocolate.
- **Apples**: A standard crop grown in the Farming Zone.

Your farming strategy should evolve as your town grows. In the early game, focus on crops that are quick to grow and have simple processing chains. Wheat is an excellent starter crop because it is versatile, cheap to plant, and its products (Flour and Bread) sell for a consistent profit.

As you unlock more advanced machines and recipes, you should shift your focus to crops that form the basis of more complex and profitable goods. Sugarcane and Coffee Beans are excellent mid-game crops because their processed forms (Sugar and Roasted Coffee Beans) are staples for many high-demand items.

In the late game, efficiency becomes paramount. By this point, you should have a fully automated farm with multiple farmhands. The best crops are those that yield the highest profit per day and per unit of space. This often involves crops like Cocoa Beans and Grapes, which can be turned into premium products like Chocolate and Grape Juice. Some players also find success converting their entire farm into production for a single, high-demand item like Popcorn, which sells for a solid price in market stands.

## Pro Tips for Maximizing Your Farm

- **Automate Early and Often**: Your goal should be to automate your farm as quickly as possible. Hire farmhands to water and harvest crops, and set up processing machines to run continuously. This frees you up to focus on other aspects of town management.
- **Manage Your Storage**: Farming can quickly fill up your storage with raw crops. Use market stands to sell excess produce automatically, or set up a logistics network to move items directly to processing machines. You can also lock items in your storage to prevent them from being used in unwanted recipes.
- **Plan Your Layout**: Place your farming zones and processing buildings close together to minimize travel time for your workers. A well-organized layout is crucial for efficient automation.
- **Balance Raw and Processed Goods**: While selling processed goods is generally more profitable, some town goals and tourist requests require raw crops. Keep a small reserve of raw crops or maintain a dedicated plot for them.
- **Expand Gradually**: As you level up your town, you will unlock more farmhands and the ability to create additional farming zones. Don't be afraid to expand, but do so methodically to ensure your workers and machines can handle the increased workload.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How do I unlock new seeds?**
New seeds are unlocked as you progress through the game and level up your town. They can usually be purchased from the market or from specific vendors that appear as your town grows.

**What is the most profitable crop to sell?**
There is no single "best" crop, as profitability depends on your town's needs and the stage of the game. However, processing raw crops into goods like Chocolate, Roasted Coffee Beans, and Grape Juice generally yields a much higher profit than selling the raw crop itself.

**Can I lose my crops if I don't water them?**
Yes, crops need to be watered to grow. While you can water them manually, hiring farmhands or building sprinklers is a much more efficient way to ensure your crops are always watered.

**What is the "Feed" item I sometimes get from harvesting?**
When harvesting crops, you have a chance to obtain an item called "Feed". This item is placed in a feed trough to feed your animals, such as cows, which then produce milk.

**How many farmhands can I have?**
The number of farmhands you can hire increases as you level up your town. For instance, an update added another farmhand at level 7. Continue to level up your town to unlock more workers for your farm.
