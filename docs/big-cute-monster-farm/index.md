# 🐾 Your Big, Cute Monster Farm Guides

> KaiPlay Studio's idle creature-farm game | Released November 10, 2025 | PC (Steam) | "Mostly Positive" (79%)

**Your Big, Cute Monster Farm** (幻兽大农场：放置好时光) is a cozy idle farming game where cute monsters automatically handle every chore — tilling, sowing, watering, and harvesting — so you can grow a thriving farm without touching a tool. It sits at the bottom of your screen like a desktop pet, keeps earning while you work, and hides in one click. This is one of the most relaxing farming games of 2025-2026, and one of the best games to run while you work from home.
