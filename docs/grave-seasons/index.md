# ⚰️ Grave Seasons Guides

> Perfect Garbage × Blumhouse Games | Released August 14, 2026 | PC, PS5, Xbox Series X|S, Nintendo Switch · Xbox Game Pass day one

**Grave Seasons** is the strangest cozy-adjacent game of 2026: a full pixel-art farming and life sim set in the small mountainside town of **Ashenridge**, where you play an escaped convict rebuilding a farm at the top of the mountain — and where one of your 30+ neighbors is secretly a **supernatural serial killer**. You have one in-game year to farm, investigate, protect victims and unmask the killer, whose identity is randomly reassigned on every playthrough.

## The Hook

Farming sims usually promise peace: a quiet valley, a watering can, neighbors who adore you. **Grave Seasons promises the opposite — and means it.** The cozy loop is all here — seasonal crops, fishing, mining, foraging, kitchen and shed crafting, 12 romanceable neighbors, a town that changes with the seasons. But the town has a body count, the residents all have hidden supernatural sides, and the person you're buying seeds from might be the one killing people after dark. Every choice is autosaved and tracked, people live or die on your decisions, and the identity of the killer is redrawn from a fresh candidate pool on every single run. It's Stardew Valley with a murder board.
