# 🦊 Tales of Seikyu Guides

> ACE Entertainment's yokai farming sim | Launched June 11, 2026 (Steam 1.0) | PC | $24.99 ($17.49 launch week)

**Tales of Seikyu** is a cozy yokai farming and life-sim game on the hidden island of Seikyu, where spirits live openly as humans. It's the farming game with no hoe and no watering can — instead you transform into yokai forms: **Boar** to till and clear, **Slime** to water, **Tengu** to fly, **Yuki-onna** to hover, and **Fox** to finish the story. Underneath the cozy farm sits a **20-candidate romance system**, an **86-room dungeon** (Fox Ruins), ranch animals, festivals, and a proper endgame.

## The Hook

Most farming games hand you a hoe and a watering can and call it a day. **Tales of Seikyu** hands you a bloodline: every tool on the farm is a yokai transformation, and the game's most efficient route is learning which form does what. It's a genuinely fresh spin on the Stardew formula — and with **20 romance candidates**, a **86-room dungeon**, and a year of Early Access of polish behind it, there's far more depth than the cozy art style suggests.
