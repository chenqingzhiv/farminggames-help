    The Complete Tales of Seikyu Fishing Guide

    Fishing in Tales of Seikyu is more than a relaxing sideline; it connects directly to cooking, villager requests, and shrine offerings. From the humble Crucian Carp to the elusive Siberian Taimen, mastering the waters around Seikyu Island is essential for any dedicated farmer. This guide covers the mechanics of tales of seikyu fishing, provides a complete seasonal fish list, highlights the best casting locations, and shares advanced strategies to help you catch every species.

    How Fishing Works

    You can unlock the fishing rod on your first day. Accept the "Teach a Yokai to Fish" request from the town board, then speak to Torleone, the local fishmonger at the docks. He will give you a Basic Rod.

    Once equipped from your tool wheel, look for dark silhouettes moving in the water—those are catchable spots. Cast your line and wait for a bite. When a fish takes the bait, a stamina gauge appears at the top of the screen. You must press the displayed keys (such as 'A' and 'D') to pull against the fish's movement, draining its stamina. When the bar empties, reel in quickly before the fish recovers.

    Fishing wears down your rod's durability over time, so keep resources on hand for repairs or upgrades. As you gain fishing experience, you unlock better bait and specialized traps.

    Complete Fish List by Season and Location

    There are 37 fish and crustaceans to catch in Tales of Seikyu, with availability tied to season, time of day, and sometimes weather. The table below lists key catches to help you plan your collection.

    Fish Name Season Location Time
    Crucian Carp All Ponds, Rivers, Ocean All Day
    Shad Spring, Summer Ponds, Rivers, Ocean All Day
    Horse Mackerel Fall, Winter Ponds, Rivers, Ocean All Day
    Mudskipper Spring Ponds, Rivers, Ocean All Day
    Sweetfish Summer Ponds, Rivers, Ocean All Day
    Black Bass All (common) Rivers (near town square) All Day
    Gar All (rain required) Rivers All Day
    Blobfish Fall, Winter (rare) Docks / Waterwheel Day/Night (post level 10)
    Rainbow Trout Fall, Winter (rare) River (behind house) All Day
    Siberian Taimen Winter (very rare) Ponds (snowy days) All Day

    Shrine Offerings and Fish

    A key late-game activity involves donating specific fish to the shrine. A past bug prevented purchased fish from counting, but that has since been patched. Current requirements involve donating multiple specimens of the same species, such as 10 Blobfish, 10 Rainbow Trout, or 3 Siberian Taimen.

    Best Fishing Spots

    While you can fish in most bodies of water, certain locations yield better results for specific species:

    • Seikyu Town Square River: A reliable spot for common catches like Black Bass and Crucian Carp.
    • Docks and Waterwheel: Prime locations for rarer fish like the Blobfish, especially once you reach a higher fishing level.
    • Behind the House (River): The area near the water wheel behind your farmhouse consistently produces Rainbow Trout in Fall and Winter.
    • Ocean (Boats): To access deeper sea fish, complete the "Big Boss Fishing Heart Event" with Torleone and rent a boat from his nephew Seabert.

    Rod Upgrades, Bait, and Yokai Form Bonuses

    To truly excel at tales of seikyu fishing, you need to leverage all available tools and upgrades.

    • Fishing Skill Rewards: As your fishing level increases, you unlock better bait. Notable milestones include:

      • Level 1: Bug Bait Box & Basic Fish Bait
      • Level 3: Rare Fish Bait
      • Level 5: Bountiful Catch Bait & Rainy Day Bait
      • Level 7: Special Fish Bait
      • Level 8: Nighttime Bait
      • Level 10: Treasure Hunter Bait

    • Bait Strategy: Using the correct bait is mandatory for certain catches. While basic bait works for common fish, specialized bait allows you to target specific species or even fish up sunken treasure chests.

    • The Kappa Form: Specific Yokai bonuses are not fully detailed for fishing, but completing the "Teach a Yokai to Fish" quest introduces Prince the Kappa, who gives you the "Kappa's Keepsake." This suggests a deep link between your Yokai forms and aquatic life, likely offering passive fishing efficiency bonuses as you advance Kappa-related friendship events.

    Pro Tips

    • Keep a stock of common fish. Do not sell everything. Villager requests like "Fish Please!" may ask for multiple specimens of a single species. Keeping 5–10 common fish on hand saves you from scrambling later.

    • Watch the weather. Some fish, like the Gar, only appear on rainy days. When rain is forecast, prioritize fishing over other chores.

    • Adjust expectations in Winter. Many players report that Winter is a slow season for profits, with Horse Mackerel appearing overwhelmingly often. Use this time to hunt for rare winter catches like the Blobfish and Taimen, but do not rely on fishing for steady income.

    • Check the water wheel frequently. If you are hunting for Rainbow Trout, the pond by the water wheel behind your house is one of the few verified spots for consistent spawns.

    Fishing in Tales of Seikyu rewards patience and preparation. By learning the seasonal patterns, using the right bait, and watching the weather, you will catch all 37 species and complete your shrine offerings in due time.
